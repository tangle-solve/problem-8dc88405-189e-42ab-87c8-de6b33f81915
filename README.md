### ALGO-000167: A factor-two approximation for shortest common superstring
- **Subfield:** String algorithms and compression
- **Open:** Give a polynomial-time algorithm producing a common superstring of length at most twice optimum.
- **Status:** General approximation remains above two in the cited literature. Shibata’s September 2026 preprint reports that the classical greedy algorithm can have ratio at least \(9/4\); this defeats that proposed route, not the general factor-two target.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would improve a central overlap-based sequence assembly problem.
- **Key works:** Nikolaev (2024), arXiv:2407.20422; Shibata (2026), *Disproving the Greedy Superstring Conjecture*, arXiv:2609.01365.
- **Related:** Bioinformatics—genome assembly.
